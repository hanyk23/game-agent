### Preserved execution boundaries

- Manifest 1.2/Assembly 1.1/Graph 1.2 provide scoped dependencies, admitted
  assets, exact authorities, and ready execution without raw kernel authority.
- The Node-only loader rejects imports, ambient authority, dynamic evaluation,
  unexpected exports, byte drift, and top-level executable statements before
  minting a private handle over exact self-contained ESM bytes.
- Browser catalogs are frozen evidence. Node hashing, registry admission,
  filesystem, environment, and path authority stay outside the H5 runtime.
- One transition guard rejects lifecycle/frame/event/observation reentry before
  side effects. Timers dispatch first; participant updates remain provider-first;
  nested synchronous events are inherited and bounded.
- Factories receive only exact semantic services, ports, clock, and assets. They
  receive no raw leases, engine objects, registry, resolver, Phaser, filesystem,
  environment, descriptors, or runtime handles.
- Entity and contact mutation reserve conservative quarantine capacity. Contact
  prepare is pure; commit mutates the source first; indeterminate custody emits
  no gameplay result and blocks verification and packaging.

- Hostile contention keeps every delivery's physical pool and channel independent
  while one host-owned group enforces active-entity, active-projectile, and
  trailing-1,000-ms spawn capacity with deterministic stable suffix drops.

- The singleton score ledger accepts bounded finite non-negative binary64 awards,
  preserves legal fractional values such as `7.5`, rejects duplicate evidence,
  and performs one stable ordinary addition without rounding or coercion.
- Outcome conditions only refresh retained state. A host-only post-provider/

- Production V1.4 registry lookup and catalog generation bind exact manifest/
